**The best places to find Forward-Deployed Architect/Engineer (FDE) jobs combine specialized FDE-focused boards with major general platforms and direct company career pages.** These roles are concentrated at AI labs, enterprise software companies (e.g., Palantir, Databricks, Salesforce, OpenAI, Anthropic), and growing numbers of startups and consultancies, so targeted searches work better than broad ones.

### Specialized FDE Job Boards (Highest Signal)
These sites aggregate or curate “Forward Deployed,” deployment, applied AI, and related customer-facing engineering roles, often with filters, salary data, and direct links to official postings:

- **DeployedJobs** (deployedjobs.com) — Dedicated board for Forward Deployed, Solutions, Applied AI, and Implementation engineering roles at AI labs and enterprise platforms.  
- **fde.directory** (fde.directory) — Tracks FDE postings, salaries, and related resources; focuses on the growing FDE market.  
- **FDE Pulse** (fdepulse.com) — Tracks 50+ companies and hundreds of open FDE roles with salary insights and market data.  
- **forwardeployedjobs.com** (or similar “Forward Deployed Jobs”) — Aggregates thousands of forward-deployed, deployment, and solutions engineering listings from public ATS feeds (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, etc.), refreshed frequently.  
- **fde.directory / FD Roles / FDE Jobs List** variants and similar niche sites (e.g., fdejobslist.com, fdroles.com) — Curated lists focused specifically on “Deployed” titles.  
- Other emerging ones: Rung, Plank, FDEnest job sections, and startup.jobs filters for FDE roles.

These specialized boards are particularly useful because the title varies (Forward Deployed Engineer, FDSE, Applied AI Engineer, Deployment Engineer, Solutions Architect with deployment focus, etc.).

### Major General Platforms
- **LinkedIn Jobs** — One of the strongest overall sources. Search exact phrases like “Forward Deployed Engineer,” “Forward Deployed Architect,” “FDSE,” or “Forward Deployed” + company names. Use Easy Apply, set alerts, and leverage network referrals (highly effective for these roles).  
- **Indeed**, **Glassdoor**, and **Levels.fyi** (for salary context + some listings).  
- **Wellfound** (formerly AngelList) and **Y Combinator’s Work at a Startup** — Strong for startup and early-stage FDE openings.  
- **Greenhouse / Lever / Ashby / Workday company boards** — Many roles appear first (or only) on the employer’s ATS; specialized boards scrape these.

### Direct Company Career Pages (Often Best for Top Roles)
Check these frequently, as many high-paying FDE positions (especially at frontier labs) post primarily or exclusively here:
- OpenAI, Anthropic, Palantir, Databricks, Salesforce, Scale AI, Google Cloud, AWS, Microsoft, Ramp, and similar AI/enterprise firms.
- Consulting firms (Deloitte, Accenture, etc.) that run FDE or Forward Deployed Engineering practices.

### Additional Tips
- Set up keyword alerts on LinkedIn and specialized boards for variations: “Forward Deployed,” “FDE,” “FDSE,” “Deployment Engineer,” “Applied AI Engineer,” “Customer-Facing Engineer,” “Implementation Engineer.”  
- Network and referrals matter a lot—Palantir alumni, AI lab networks, and specialized recruiters (e.g., Betts Recruiting, Paraform, Recruiting from Scratch) frequently fill these roles.  
- Government/defense-related FDE roles may also appear on ClearanceJobs or company pages requiring security clearances.  
- Remote/hybrid options exist but many still involve significant travel or on-site embedding.

Start with the specialized boards + LinkedIn alerts + direct checks of the major AI/enterprise employers for the highest-quality, most current listings. The market has grown rapidly, so checking multiple sources weekly is recommended.
