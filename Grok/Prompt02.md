**Forward-Deployed Engineer (FDE) / Architect salaries in the US (as of mid-to-late 2026) typically range from roughly $160K–$220K in base pay for most roles, with total compensation (base + bonus + equity) often $200K–$550K+ depending on level, company, and equity value.** Top packages at frontier AI labs can exceed $700K–$1M+ at senior/staff levels.

Compensation varies widely by experience, company tier, location, and especially equity (which can make up 40–70% of total pay at AI companies). Figures are drawn from job postings, Levels.fyi, Glassdoor, H-1B data, and compensation reports.

### Overall US Ranges
- **Median base salary**: ~$174K–$200K (commonly $160K–$220K for the middle 50% of postings).
- **Indeed average base**: ~$173K (range ~$111K–$271K).
- **Levels.fyi median total compensation**: ~$205K (25th–75th percentile roughly $175K–$278K; 90th ~$334K).
- **By experience level** (approximate US totals, including equity):
  | Level              | Base Salary     | Typical Total Comp    |
  |--------------------|-----------------|-----------------------|
  | Entry-level       | $120K–$160K    | $140K–$200K          |
  | Mid-level (2–5 yrs)| $160K–$220K   | $220K–$320K (up to $385K at top firms) |
  | Senior (5+ yrs)   | $200K–$280K    | $300K–$450K+         |
  | Staff/Lead/Principal | $250K–$350K+ | $450K–$700K+ (up to $1M+ at frontier labs) |

### By Company Tier / Notable Employers
**Highest-paying (frontier AI labs)** — OpenAI and Anthropic lead:
- Base often $160K–$320K.
- Estimated total comp: $350K–$550K for mid-to-senior roles; staff/principal levels $600K–$1M+ (equity-heavy, often 55–70% of package).

**Palantir** (originator of the role, often titled Forward Deployed Software Engineer/FDSE):
- Base typically $135K–$200K.
- Median total compensation ~$211K–$215K (range roughly $171K–$295K; higher at staff levels, sometimes $400K+).

**Other examples**:
- Databricks: Base ~$182K–$250K; total up to $200K–$500K+.
- Google / AWS / Microsoft: Broad bands, often $100K–$300K+ base depending on level.
- Salesforce, Ramp, and many startups: Competitive bases in the $150K–$330K range, plus equity.
- Consulting firms (e.g., Deloitte) and enterprises: Can reach high bases ($200K+) but often lower equity.

**Architect variants** (e.g., Principal Forward Deployed Architect at companies like Atlassian) tend to sit at the senior/staff end of these ranges, reflecting greater experience and strategic focus.

### Key Factors Influencing Pay
- **Equity** is the biggest differentiator, especially at private AI companies where valuations drive large upside (or risk).
- **Location**: Premiums in San Francisco, New York, Washington DC, and other tech hubs; remote roles can be slightly lower.
- **Travel / embedding demands**: Roles with significant customer on-site work often command a premium.
- **AI specialization**: Stronger demand and higher pay for AI/ML-focused FDEs.

Salaries are highly variable and change with market conditions, stock performance, and individual negotiation. For the most current figures, check Levels.fyi, recent job postings with pay transparency, or Glassdoor for specific companies. Total compensation packages frequently include benefits, bonuses, and equity beyond base salary.
